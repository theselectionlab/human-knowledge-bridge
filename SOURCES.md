## Deliberate omissions

These are accurate but were left out of child-facing copy on purpose:

- **Crust thickness numbers.** NASA's pages give different figures (about 40/60 km and
  about 70/150 km) for how thick the crust is on each side. They agree on the
  direction, not the size, so the demo says only "thicker on the far side" and puts no
  number in front of a child.
- **Core radius and other interior numbers.** Too detailed for the five-screen flow,
  and not needed for the concept.
- **A museum-style instrument spec sheet.** The demo sticks to what the child needs in
  order to understand what was measured.

## Attribution rules followed

- Source links name the NASA page and open in a new tab.
- The demo never writes "NASA says" or "according to NASA". Facts are attributed by
  linking to the page that states them.
- AI-drafted text is never presented as NASA's own words.
